Lapdroid v0.3.0:
- Full keyboard keycode coverage (A-Z, 0-9, F1-F12, full numpad, navigation).
- Synchronized modifier states (Shift, AltGr, Ctrl, Caps Lock).
- Desktop keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, word jumps/deletion).
- Shift text selection (Shift+Arrows, Shift+Home/End).
- Configurable Enter and Tab modes in settings.
- Optional direct input IME for Termux and games.
- Live diagnostic logging.
