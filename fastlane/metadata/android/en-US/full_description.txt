Lapdroid turns your Windows laptop or PC keyboard, touchpad, and active stylus into a high-precision Bluetooth input controller for Android phones and tablets.

NO WI-FI OR NETWORK REQUIRED:
Operates 100% offline over direct Bluetooth RFCOMM. Your keystrokes and pointer actions never touch the internet or local Wi-Fi network.

KEY FEATURES:
• Full Keyboard Forwarding: Complete coverage for letters, numbers, F1-F12, numpad, navigation keys, and international layouts.
• Modifier Synchronization: Synchronized Shift, Ctrl, Alt, AltGr, and CapsLock states.
• Desktop Shortcuts: Full support for standard shortcuts like Ctrl+A (Select All), Ctrl+C (Copy), Ctrl+V (Paste), Ctrl+X (Cut), Ctrl+Z (Undo), Ctrl+Y (Redo), and word jumping/deletion.
• Shift Text Selection: Seamlessly highlight text with Shift+Arrows and Shift+Home/End.
• Touchpad & Pointer Emulation: Smooth custom floating cursor overlay with left, right, and middle click emulation, multi-touch gestures, and natural scroll.
• Stylus Digitizer: Direct absolute stylus coordinates and pressure forwarding for drawing and note-taking.
• Configurable Controls: Smart Enter key actions, Tab focus navigation, and Super key mappings.
• Optional Direct IME: Built-in Input Method Service for terminal emulators (Termux) and gaming.
• Zero Internet Access: Does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission.
